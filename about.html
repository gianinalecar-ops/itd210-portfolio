Who We Are

Guidance that helps youth move forward.

Breaking Cycles International supports youth and families through mentorship, faith-centered guidance, and practical tools that encourage better choices, stronger relationships, and lasting change.

Our Mission

Purpose. Dignity. Direction.

We help young people see their value, build discipline, and choose a future that is not defined by the cycles around them.

Why We Exist

Breaking Cycles International was created to interrupt patterns of violence, instability, and hopelessness by providing mentorship, faith-based guidance, and practical life tools.

We believe change happens through relationships - through consistent support, accountability, and love.

“We don’t just interrupt cycles. We replace them with vision.”
