Resources to Help You Break Cycles

Whether you're seeking support, healing, or a fresh start, these resources are here to guide you forward.

Support Resources

Community Outreach

Access food banks, housing assistance, and emergency support programs that meet immediate needs in your community.

  • Food banks & meal programs
  • Emergency housing support
  • Financial assistance resources

Mentorship & Growth

Find mentorship programs and personal development resources that provide guidance, accountability, and encouragement.

  • 1-on-1 mentoring
  • Life coaching
  • Accountability groups

Education & Career

Explore GED programs, job training, and career-building tools designed to help you create long-term stability.

  • GED prep and testing support
  • Job training & certification programs
  • College and trade school guidance

Family Support

Strengthen your home with parenting resources, counseling services, and family support programs.

  • Housing stability and emergency family assistance
  • Child development and early learning resources
  • Mental health and emotional wellness support

Support That Meets You Where You Are

At Breaking Cycles, we provide resources designed to uplift individuals and families through every stage of their journey. Whether you need mentorship, educational support, or guidance for your family, we are here to walk alongside you.

Our programs are rooted in compassion, growth, and real transformation—because no one should have to break cycles alone.

Immediate Help

If you are in crisis or need urgent support, please use the resources below:

  • Call or text 988 – Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
  • Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233
  • Emergency: 911

Need More Support?

Our team is here to help connect you with the right resources and walk alongside you on your journey.
